When RyzikOS hits an error it can't recover from, it now shows a blue screen that says what went wrong, instead of freezing with red text.
The blue screen saves a report on the disk and restarts the computer. After you sign in, a window explains why the computer restarted, with every detail a click away.
The Terminal commands "crash" (show the last report) and "panic" (try the blue screen) go with it.
