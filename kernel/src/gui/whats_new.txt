VPN: a new app that works like Happ. Paste the subscription address your VPN service gave you, or a vless://, trojan:// or ss:// link, pick a server and press the big button.
It speaks VLESS with REALITY or TLS (with XTLS Vision too), Trojan and Shadowsocks, and shows each server's delay, your traffic and when the subscription ends.
While the VPN is on, every program goes through it, the menu bar shows a green shield, and Quick settings has a VPN tile.
