Moving the mouse and windows is smoother now: RyzikOS redraws only the part of the screen that changed, and window shadows and edges draw about three times faster.
In a virtual machine the pointer no longer jumps a pixel or two after every move.
If fullscreen looks stretched, open Settings > System > Resolution and pick a size with the shape of your monitor, such as 1920 x 1200 for a 16:10 screen. The Terminal command "screen 1920x1200" does the same, and RyzikOS keeps your choice.
