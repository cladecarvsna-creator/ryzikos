Telegram shows photos and files in the chat. Click one to download it to Downloads and open it.
Emoji are drawn as pictures, and the smiley next to the message box opens an emoji picker.
Links in messages can be clicked: web pages open in the browser, @names and t.me links open the chat.
Search finds people and channels on Telegram too. Channels you are not in can be read and joined.
Click the chat's name at the top for its profile: its @name and link to copy, its description, Join or Leave.
Right-click a message to copy its text or link, or to save its file.
