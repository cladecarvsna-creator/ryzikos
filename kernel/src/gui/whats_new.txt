Windows now have a green button next to close and minimise: it fills the screen with the window, and pressing it again puts the window back. A double-click on the title bar, Super+Up or dragging a window to the top of the screen does the same; Super+Down or pulling the title bar puts it back.
Windows can be resized: hold the mouse on a corner or an edge (the pointer turns into arrows) and drag. Files, the Browser, Telegram, the Terminal and the other apps lay themselves out for the new size.
Small windows like Calculator, About and Draw keep their size, so their green button is dim.
