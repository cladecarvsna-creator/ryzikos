New app: Archiver. Make ZIP archives that open in 7-Zip, WinRAR and Windows, and open ZIP, TAR, TAR.GZ and GZIP files.
Your files are on the left and the archive on the right: tick files and press Add to pack them, or tick files in the archive and press Extract. Each file shows how much it was packed.
In Files, double-click an archive to open it, or right-click for "Add to ZIP archive" and "Extract to folder". The Terminal has zip and unzip too.
